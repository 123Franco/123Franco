# Hello. I see you've found me. ☕

Interesting.

Of all the places you could be on the internet, you've ended up here. Perhaps you're looking for code. Perhaps you're looking for answers. Or perhaps you're simply curious.

I like curious people.

### A little about me

I'm a software developer. I build things, solve problems, and occasionally make computers do something rather clever.

I find that programming and reading people have quite a lot in common.

Both involve patterns. Both require patience. And in both cases, the smallest details tend to reveal the most.

My work takes me through **mobile applications, backend systems, AI, automation, and whatever else catches my attention.**

I don't particularly care for complicated solutions to simple problems. Elegance, you see, is knowing what to leave out.

### What I'm working on

A few experiments. Some useful applications. A couple of ideas that probably shouldn't work, but might.

I enjoy building things that make people stop and say:

*"Oh. That's clever."*

That's usually a good sign.

### My philosophy

- **Observe first.** Most problems tell you how to solve them if you pay attention.
- **Question assumptions.** Especially the ones everyone agrees on.
- **Keep it simple.** Complexity is often just confusion dressed in expensive clothes.
- **Stay curious.** There's always something interesting hiding in plain sight.

### One last thing...

People tend to think the secret is knowing more.

It isn't.

It's noticing more.

Anyway, have a look around. You might find something interesting.

And if you have an intriguing idea, a difficult problem, or simply something worth discussing, do get in touch.

I do enjoy a good mystery.

**Until then,**  
*Keep your eyes open.* ☕
